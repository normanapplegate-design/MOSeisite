MOSei -- Gap Scoring Tool
An observer-verified diagnostic instrument for consultants deploying the Norman System with clients. Score external pressure against internal regulation across four dimensions, with evidence-based scoring discipline at every question.
WHY

Self-assessments are useful but biased -- a plant manager scoring their own system tends toward optimism. This tool is built for an outside observer to score what the evidence actually shows, producing a defensible, client-ready Gap Score.

Purpose

Score 16 questions across four dimensions of Norman's Gap -- External Pressure, Leadership & Ownership, Signal Quality, and Operational Systems. Produces a Gap Index, Gap Score, Gap Severity band, and Effectiveness %.

Inputs

Observer scoring based on direct evidence -- Gemba observations, document review, interviews, and KPI data. Not a self-report. Each question requires a cited observation, not an impression.

Outputs

Gap Index (0-10 scale), Gap Score (External minus Internal), Gap Severity band (Stable / Early Strain / High Risk / Critical), Effectiveness % (Internal capacity as a percentage of pressure), dimension breakdown, and a printable one-page client summary.

Cadence

Run at engagement start as the baseline, then quarterly or at major milestones to show trend. A single score is a diagnosis -- a series of scores is evidence of whether the engagement is working.

Escalation Path

Gap Score above +2.0 (High Risk or Critical) warrants immediate executive briefing. Don't wait for the full engagement report -- a Gap this wide is already producing operational failures somewhere in the system.

Supporting Documents

Pairs with the MOS Pressure Test for stress-scenario modeling, and the MOS Assessment Tool if the client wants a self-assessment baseline to compare against your observer score.

Three Things Most Likely to Fail in Year One
1
Scoring from impression, not evidence. Every score should trace to something you saw, read, or heard directly -- not a general sense of how the plant feels.
2
Letting the client see the score before the evidence. Walk through what you observed first. The number should feel inevitable, not surprising.
3
Treating one score as the whole engagement. The Gap Score opens the conversation. The dimension breakdown and recommendations are where the value is.

Four dimensions, 16 questions

1
External Pressure -- market, regulatory, competitive, and supply chain forces on this site.
2
Leadership & Ownership -- clarity of accountability and decision authority.
3
Signal Quality -- how cleanly information travels from floor to leadership.
4
Operational Systems -- standard work, routines, and the plant's capacity to absorb change.

Score each question 1-10 based on direct observation. Takes approximately 20-30 minutes including evidence review.
