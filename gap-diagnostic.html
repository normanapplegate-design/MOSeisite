Where Is Your Organization Breaking Down?
Most assessments ask if you're ready. This measures exactly where the gap is. We call it the Gap Diagnostic. Slider-based scoring across external pressure, system regulation, and leader regulation. Produces four outputs: Gap Score, Gap Index, Gap Severity, and Effectiveness %.
What this tool measures

Norman's Gap is the structural distance between external pressure and internal regulation. This tool measures that gap with precision — using a tier-weighted severity score for pressure and a composite of system and leader regulation for response capacity.

The result is four numbers that together tell you not just whether a gap exists, but how wide it is, how fast it may be growing, and where the regulation breakdown is occurring.

The five Gap Index bands
Stable (below 1.0)Regulation exceeds pressure. Reserve capacity exists.
Managed tension (1.0–1.15)Pressure and regulation close. Monitor the trend.
Widening gap (1.16–1.30)Pressure outpacing regulation. Intervention warranted now.
Instability forming (1.31–1.50)Gap is material. Visible to anyone looking. Act now.
System risk (above 1.50)Regulation overwhelmed. Outcomes degrading. Stabilize first.
What this tool scores
1
External pressure — Tier classification (behavioral, operational, or system-level) multiplied by severity (6.0–9.0 scale).
2
System regulation — Four factors scored: ownership clarity, decision speed, escalation discipline, and KPI truth.
3
Leader regulation — Three sliders: self-regulation under pressure, decision composure, and trade-off discipline.
4
Results — Gap Score (pressure minus regulation), Gap Index (pressure divided by regulation), Severity band, Effectiveness %, and the single weakest constraint — ownership, decision speed, or information quality.

The Gap Diagnostic is a management decision model, not a precision instrument. Weightings, tier multipliers, and band cutoffs reflect deliberate design choices, not statistical validation against a dataset of organizations. Scores are directional indicators meant to identify relative operating risk and where to investigate first — not exact measurements.

Based on Norman’s Gap, Signal Compression, and the Norman Decision Window frameworks by Norman Applegate. Published by MOSei.org.
