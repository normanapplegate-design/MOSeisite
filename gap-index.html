What is the Gap Index?

The Gap Index is a ratio: External Pressure divided by Internal Regulation. A score below 1.0 means regulation exceeds pressure — the system has reserve capacity. Above 1.0 means pressure is outpacing the system’s ability to respond. The higher the number, the wider the gap and the greater the instability risk.

Unlike the Plant Change Readiness Assessment (which diagnoses the structural condition of a site), the Gap Index Scorer measures the real-time condition of a system under a specific pressure — and includes a leader regulation component, because how a leader behaves under pressure is part of the system.

The five Gap Index bands
Stable (below 1.0)Regulation exceeds pressure. Reserve capacity exists.
Managed tension (1.0–1.15)Pressure and regulation close. Monitor the trend.
Widening gap (1.16–1.30)Pressure outpacing regulation. Intervention warranted now.
Instability forming (1.31–1.50)Gap is material. Visible to anyone looking. Act now.
System risk (above 1.50)Regulation overwhelmed. Outcomes degrading. Stabilize first.
What this tool scores

The assessment has four sections scored in about ten minutes.

1
External pressure — Consequence level and how confirmed the pressure is across the organization.
2
System regulation — Ownership clarity, decision speed, escalation discipline, and KPI truth. The structural capacity of the organization to respond.
3
Leader regulation — Self-regulation under pressure, decision composure, and trade-off discipline. How the leader’s own behavior amplifies or absorbs system stress.
4
Direction — Whether the gap has been widening, holding, or closing over the last several weeks.
