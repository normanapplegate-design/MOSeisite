Leading vs. Lagging, On Purpose

A lagging indicator tells you what already happened. A leading indicator gives someone on the floor a chance to change the outcome before it happens. Most cascades are built almost entirely from lagging metrics, easy to report, useless for actually preventing the miss. The workshop forces at least one leading indicator into every tier of the cascade, tied to an action someone can actually take that shift.
